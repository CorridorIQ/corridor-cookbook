# Monthly Portfolio Performance Report, July 2026

## Portfolio Summary

The portfolio held steady in July across all eight multifamily assets. Blended occupancy came in at 94.0%, roughly flat versus June, and blended rent growth held at 1.3%, consistent with the pace we have seen since the spring lease-up push. Combined NOI for the month was $1,556,250, up modestly from June on the back of stable renewals and no material turnover events. Delinquency stayed inside the 0.9% to 3.1% range property by property, with the portfolio average at 1.9%, in line with trend. No property required a watchlist flag this month.

## Property Callouts

**Founders Row** posted the strongest renewal quarter in the portfolio, with rent growth accelerating to 2.4% and occupancy holding above 97%. The unit mix continues to outperform the submarket; leasing recommends holding the current asking-rent schedule into August rather than pulling back to chase volume.

**Harbor Point Commons** remains the anchor asset by NOI contribution at $412,300 for the month. Occupancy ticked up to 95.8% on the back of two large corporate leases signed in June that moved in on schedule. No action needed; continue current renewal offer cadence.

**Thistledown Commons** is the softest performer in the book on occupancy (90.8%) and rent growth (0.5%), though delinquency and NOI both held flat month over month. Asset management is reviewing concession strategy for August to close the occupancy gap before it widens further.

## Outlook

August should be a quiet month absent any known move-outs on the calendar, though Aldergate Yards has a cluster of lease expirations landing mid-month that warrants a closer look in next month's report. We expect blended occupancy and rent growth to hold near July levels, with NOI tracking modestly upward on continued renewal strength at Founders Row and Harbor Point Commons. No portfolio-level concerns at this time.
